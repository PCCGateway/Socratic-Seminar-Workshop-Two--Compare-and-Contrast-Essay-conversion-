Your Task

You have researched, compared perspectives, and prepared for a Socratic seminar. Now you will turn those ideas into a formal compare-and-contrast essay.

ESSENTIAL QUESTION
How does online/technology-enhanced communication compare with in-person communication?
The goal: Do not simply copy your seminar notes. Develop them into a clear argument supported by research, explanation, and comparison.

8. Sources & MLA Citations

Add the sources you actually used. The activity will place these on a separate Works Cited page. Check each citation against your instructor's MLA requirements before submitting.

In-text reminder: Introduce evidence with a signal phrase or parenthetical citation. Example: According to Smith, ... or ... (Smith 24). Every source cited in the essay should appear on Works Cited.
